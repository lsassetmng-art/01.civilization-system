# AIWorkerOS Policy Append: Model Capability Card

status: active
phase: model capability card
scope: AIWorkerOS only

## Public display policy

The app-facing robot selection card may show:

- high-function
- business-specialized
- high-precision review
- complex-work capable
- strong complementary proposal
- conversation-specialized
- time-axis specialized
- stable general-purpose

It must not show:

- direct other-company superiority
- hidden ranking
- unsafe capability claims
- external execution permission
- PG apply permission
- destructive action permission

## Capability display boundary

Capability badges are descriptive metadata.

They do not grant:

- additional DB permissions
- app permissions
- entitlement
- contract rights
- external execution authority
- safety boundary relaxation
